# The 50/30/20 budgeting rule

The 50/30/20 rule is a simple way to split your take-home income each month:

- **50% for needs:** rent or housing, utilities, groceries, transportation, insurance, minimum loan payments and healthcare.
- **30% for wants:** eating out, entertainment, shopping, travel and subscriptions.
- **20% for savings and debt:** an emergency fund, extra debt repayments and saving for goals.

How to use it with PathWise:

1. Look at your total income for the month in your monthly summary.
2. Multiply it by 0.5, 0.3 and 0.2 to get your three targets.
3. Compare them with your spending by category. Housing, utilities, groceries, transportation and healthcare count as needs; food, entertainment, shopping and travel are usually wants.
4. If wants are above 30%, the biggest categories there are the easiest places to cut.

The split is a starting point, not a strict rule. In expensive cities needs often take more than 50%.
