# Ways to cut spending

Start with the data: your monthly summary lists spending by category and the change from the previous month. The biggest categories and the biggest increases are where small changes matter most.

- **Food and eating out:** set a weekly limit, cook in batches and keep delivery for special occasions.
- **Shopping:** wait 48 hours before non-essential purchases and unsubscribe from sale emails.
- **Subscriptions and recurring payments:** check your recurring transactions every few months and cancel what you no longer use.
- **Transportation:** combine trips, compare fuel prices or use public transport for regular journeys.
- **Utilities:** compare providers once a year and turn off devices on standby.
- **Travel and entertainment:** plan these in advance and give them their own monthly amount so they don't push you over budget.

If you go over budget, check which categories rose compared with last month before cutting everywhere.
