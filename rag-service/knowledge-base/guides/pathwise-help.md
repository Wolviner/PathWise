# How PathWise works

**Accounts.** You can have several accounts (current or savings). One account is the default account. New transactions use it unless you pick another.

**Transactions.** Each transaction is income or an expense, with an amount, date, category, description and account. You can add one by hand or scan a receipt: PathWise reads the receipt image with AI and fills in the amount, date, merchant and category for you to check.

**Recurring transactions.** Mark a transaction as recurring and choose daily, weekly, monthly or yearly. PathWise creates the next copy automatically when it is due and updates the account balance.

**Budget.** You set one monthly budget. It is compared with the expenses on your default account for the current month. When you have used 80% of it, PathWise emails you a budget alert (at most once a month).

**Monthly report.** On the first day of each month PathWise emails a report with your income, expenses, spending by category and three AI insights.

**Copilot.** The Copilot answers questions about your own records. It refreshes its knowledge a few minutes after you add, edit or delete transactions, and every night. It shows the sources it used under each answer. It does not give investment advice.
