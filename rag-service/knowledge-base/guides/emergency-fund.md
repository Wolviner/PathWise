# Building an emergency fund

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected costs such as a medical bill, a car repair or losing your job.

- **How much:** a common target is 3 to 6 months of essential expenses. Use your monthly summaries to see what your needs (housing, utilities, groceries, transportation, healthcare) cost in a typical month.
- **Where to keep it:** somewhere safe and easy to reach, such as a separate savings account. In PathWise you can create a SAVINGS account to track it separately from your everyday account.
- **How to build it:** start small. Even 5-10% of each paycheck adds up. A recurring transaction in PathWise can move a fixed amount on payday so you don't forget.
- **When to use it:** only for real emergencies. Refill it afterwards.

Build the emergency fund before taking on extra spending goals.
